## Locked construction and generalized-role policy

The approved rule remains unchanged:

- exact concrete `Składnia` labels directly verify `case`, `preposition-case`, `infinitive`, or `clause` constructions;
- generalized `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, `GDZIE`, and `KTÓRĘDY` verify selected semantic positions but not one exclusive concrete preposition;
- same-sense concrete forms may be recorded only as narrowed realizations;
- collocations alone do not establish selection;
- generalized roles and adverbial positions do not become new model types.

Batch 5 applies this rule in three places. `mieszkać` directly selects `GDZIE` and optional `z KIM`; `w + Locative` is only one documented place realization. Appointment-sense `umówić się` directly selects `GDZIE/KIEDY`; `do + Genitive` is a documented venue/service realization, not exclusive government. Telephone `dzwonić` directly selects `DOKĄD`; documented `do` and `na` call targets are realizations, while `w sprawie` lacks independent syntax evidence.

## Meaning and construction risks

### Viewing

`oglądać` sense 2 directly lists `KOGO/CO + (w CZYM)`. The optional `w` position must be glossed narrowly as the presentation context in which the viewed person/content appears. It is not a license to mark every cinema, television, device, medium, or viewing-place phrase as selected.

`obejrzeć` independently lists only `CO` in the authorized film sense. Importing the imperfective optional `w` position would violate aspect independence.

### Completion and cessation

`skończyć` sense 1 has Accusative and infinitive, while separate colloquial sense 4 has `z + Instrumental`. Merging them would incorrectly allow the cessation frame in ordinary task-completion patterns. Conversely, discarding the direct infinitive or direct `z` evidence would violate completeness.

### Desire and request

`chcieć` sense 1 includes distinct schemas for an object, the subject's intended action, and a desired event/state. Sense 3 separately encodes a first-person polite request or intention. The two senses must not become one unrestricted template. Directly listed adverbial `JAK` remains outside the locked architecture.

### Broad `robić`

WSJP separates making/creating from performing a noun-named activity even though both use exact Accusative `CO`. The product should preserve that semantic split. Lists such as `robić pranie`, `robić zakupy`, and `robić analizę` illustrate one activity-object schema; they do not create separate governed frames for every noun.

### Content and person understanding

`rozumieć` content-comprehension sense 1 directly supports Accusative content, a `że` clause, and an interrogative-dependent clause. Empathic person-understanding sense 4 separately supports Accusative person. Shared case morphology does not erase the semantic boundary. `How`, `why`, `what`, and similar content remain values of one interrogative-dependent clause type.

### Residence

`mieszkać` is safe only with narrowing. `w + Locative` cannot be described as exclusive government from generalized `GDZIE`, particularly because same-sense connections show several concrete place strategies. Optional `z + Instrumental`, however, is directly selected as a co-resident participant. Incidental accompaniment must not be mislabeled as co-residence.

### Appointment and agreement

`umówić się` has two separate sense inventories. Appointment sense 1 supports optional partner, optional `na` appointment/activity, and generalized place/time positions, including reciprocal plural variants. Agreement sense 2 supports partner plus exact `co do`, `na`, `o`, or clause alternatives. A maximal frame combining appointment `do`, agreement `co do/o`, all clauses, partner, place, and time would be false.

### Telephone communication

Telephone `dzwonić` has generalized target `DOKĄD` and direct `że`/`żeby` content alternatives. `do + Genitive` and `na + Accusative` are documented target realizations, not exclusive government. `w sprawie + Genitive` appears only in connections; pragmatic commonness does not establish it as a selected topic frame.
