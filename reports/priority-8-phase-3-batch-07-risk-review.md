## Locked construction and generalized-role policy

- exact concrete `Składnia` labels directly verify `case`, `preposition-case`, `infinitive`, or `clause` constructions;
- generalized `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, `GDZIE`, and `KTÓRĘDY` verify selected semantic positions but not one exclusive concrete preposition;

## Meaning and construction risks

### Modal verbs and sentence packaging

`Móc` ability and permission senses each directly select an infinitive. WSJP's question-use sense also lists an infinitive, but a request such as `Czy mogę…?` does not create a special question complement. `Czy`, word order, interrogative intonation, person, and politeness are sentence grammar and pragmatics.

`Musieć` core necessity directly selects an infinitive in both unrestricted-subject and zero-subject schemas. The zero-subject realization is not an extra complement type. Advice, expectation, criticism, and elliptical discourse uses stay outside the core record.

### Knowledge content

`Wiedzieć` directly supports Accusative content, `o + Locative` topic, `że` content, and interrogative-dependent content. A `czy` clause is a realization of the existing interrogative-dependent clause construction, not a separate fifth model type. Optional generalized source `SKĄD` is selected but unconcretized.

### Grammar-owned negation

`Jeść` exact positive syntax gives optional Accusative `CO` and optional Instrumental `CZYM`. Ordinary Genitive under negation must remain grammar-owned. Creating a lemma-specific `jeść + Genitive` pattern from negative sentences would falsely attribute a general Polish transformation to lexical valency. Partitive Genitive was not established by the exact inspected schema and is not used to muddy this boundary.

`Pić` ordinary consumption directly gives Accusative `CO`. No Genitive or partitive construction is inferred from quantity contexts, and container/source phrases remain outside the lexical frame.

### Love versus strong liking

`Kochać` person-love sense 1, idea/place attachment sense 2, and strong-liking sense 3 are separate. Their Accusative forms do not erase the semantic split. Sense 3 additionally has an exact infinitive for a strongly liked activity; that infinitive cannot be imported into emotional person-love.

### Apology schema optionality

`Przepraszać` directly gives required `KOGO + za CO` in one schema. The alternative `(KOGO) + że ZDANIE` makes the person optional only in that clause schema. Applying this optionality to the nominal person-plus-reason schema would be an alignment error.

### Wishing identity and content alternatives

`Życzyć` good-wish sense 1 directly gives required `KOMU + CZEGO` and alternative required `KOMU + żeby ZDANIE`. Genitive and clause content are alternatives, not cumulative. No infinitive is listed. `Życzyć sobie` remains a separate lexical identity and supplies no evidence.

### Same form across use and benefit senses

`Korzystać` resource-use sense 2 and benefit-from sense 1 independently select `z + Genitive`. The surface form is identical, but the semantic roles differ. Place, provider, availability, purpose, frequency, and result remain adjuncts.

### Non-reflexive `uczyć`

`Uczyć` was verified independently. Sense 1 separately lists `KOGO/CO + CZEGO`, `KOGO/CO + BEZOKOLICZNIK`, `KOGO/CO` plus a content clause, and `KOGO + o CZYM`. These alternatives cannot become a maximal `Accusative + Genitive + infinitive` frame. Sense 2 adds optional learner plus Genitive subject and generalized selected place. Nothing is inherited from `uczyć się`, and generalized `GDZIE` is not concretized.
