## Meaning and construction risks

### Communication schemas

`powiedzieć` sense 1 contains several alternative exact schemas. Optional Dative and `do + Genitive` addressees can occur with an Accusative object, an `o + Locative` topic, or a content clause; direct speech is separately listed without a recipient. Combining object, topic, `że`, `żeby`, and direct speech into one template would be false. Direct `JAK` remains outside the locked architecture.

### Beneficiaries

`gotować` exact sense 3 directly lists optional Dative beneficiary, so it is not inferred from meal scenarios. `dla + Genitive` appears outside the exact schema and receives no frame credit.

`rezerwować` booking sense 1 directly lists an optional Dative beneficiary/customer or an alternative optional `dla + Genitive` beneficiary/customer. These are not cumulative. Separate setting-aside sense 2 has its own `dla`/`na` relations and must not expand the booking frame.

### Emotional lexical-reflexive verbs

`cieszyć się` preserves lexical `się` and keeps `z + Genitive`, `na + Accusative`, `że`, and Instrumental as alternative cause/content constructions. The product must not present them as freely interchangeable.

`martwić się` independently supports Instrumental, `o + Accusative`, `że`, and interrogative-dependent content. The `o` frame overlaps released `bać się`, but worry/concern differs semantically from fear and the additional exact shapes are material. Product value is moderate, not weak; no replacement is triggered.

### Consent, opinion agreement, and matching

`zgadzać się` consent sense 1 supports `na + Accusative`, `żeby`, infinitive, and direct speech. Opinion-agreement sense 2 separately supports `z + Instrumental`, optional `z` with `że`, and direct speech. Matching/correspondence is a different meaning and must not be merged into either agreement record.

### Invitation destination

`zapraszać` directly lists Accusative invitee plus exact alternative `na + Accusative` or `do + Genitive`. Its additional `KOGO + GDZIE` schema cannot be converted into arbitrary concrete place government. Event/activity and destination/institution roles should remain distinct.

### Recommendation versus instruction

`polecać` recommendation sense 2 supports optional Dative plus Accusative person/item. Infinitive belongs to separate directive/instruction sense 1, where optional Dative also combines with `żeby`, direct speech, or an Accusative action noun/gerund as alternatives. Calling the sense-1 infinitive an ordinary recommendation would blur a real semantic boundary.

### Advice versus coping

`radzić` advice sense 2 is independent from lexical `radzić sobie`. The advice lemma has Dative advisee plus nominal, clause, direct-speech, or infinitive content; the infinitive schema alone makes Dative optional. No `z + Instrumental` coping construction is transferred. Its identity, meaning, construction inventory, and learner use give a strong independent-slot signal.

### `pasować` relation types

`pasować` cannot be a generic one-pattern “fit” record. Sense 1 uses `do + Genitive` for harmony/match; sense 2 uses optional `do + Genitive` or `na + Accusative` for physical fit; sense 4 uses optional Dative evaluator plus `do + Genitive` reference target; sense 5 uses optional Dative expectation holder/experiencer. The subject–experiencer treatment belongs to sense 5; match and fit meanings remain lexical frames with sense-specific roles.
