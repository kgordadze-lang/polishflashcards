## 5. Blocking issue: no example-author authority exists

Phase 1 requires `origin.kind: original` examples to carry an `authorRef` that resolves in
the private author registry to a record with `human: true`
(`priority7_tooling.py` `_validate_origin`, codes `AUTHOR_REGISTRY_DANGLING` /
`AUTHOR_NOT_HUMAN`). The Phase 2A/2B readiness checklist lists "authoring ownership are
approved" as a Phase 2B prerequisite, and no such authority was named for this phase.

Claude is the drafting agent, not a human author, so:

- no `original` example was written into the corpus;
- no author registry record was fabricated;
- the 22 drafted sentences live in the review queue, where a named human author can adopt
  them (or replace them) and a native reviewer can assess them.

The corpus is fully valid without them, because examples are optional in research records
and no activity is eligible.

## 6. Modelling boundaries discovered by real data

Two constructions that a learner-facing pilot would plausibly want turned out to be
unrepresentable in the locked Phase 1 model. Both were left **out of corpus** rather than
forced in, and both are raised for decision.

1. **`czekać, aż …`** — WSJP lists `czekać + aż ZDANIE` as a core contemporary frame. The
   closed `clauseKind` enum is `ze | czy | zeby | interrogative`; `aż` is not a member.
   Encoding it as `zeby` would be false. Nothing was authored.
2. **`mówić po polsku`** — the language-ability sense uses `po` + an adverbial form
   (`po polsku`), not a preposition + inflected case. The closed complement taxonomy has no
   adverbial slot. Nothing was authored; the `mówić` meaning scope explicitly excludes it.

These join the Phase 1 `to jest` boundary, which was respected: `być` has exactly one
pattern (Instrumental role predicate) and links to the Nominative grammar topic through a
typed `contrast` content reference rather than producing a misleading generic
`być` + Nominative record.

A third structural note: `zależeć komuś na czymś` has a Dative experiencer but **no
subject**, so it is typed `lexical-frame` with an `experiencer` role, not
`subject-experiencer` (which the validator requires to carry both `subject` and
`experiencer` roles). The relation taxonomy handled this correctly; the wording question
goes to review.
