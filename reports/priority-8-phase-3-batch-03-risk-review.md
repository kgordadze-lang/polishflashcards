## Meaning and schema risks

### `zapominać`

Recall sense 1 directly supports Genitive/Accusative object, `o + Locative`, `że`, and an interrogative dependent clause. Failure-to-act sense 4 separately supports an infinitive. Neither inspected schema lists `żeby`; later authoring must not merge all forms into one interchangeable pattern. This material correction requires `KEEP WITH NARROWING`.

### `próbować`

Attempt sense 1 directly supports both infinitive and Genitive action noun. Sampling sense 3 separately supports a Genitive sampled object. The shared case form must not be used to erase the difference between trying to perform an action and tasting a thing.

### `pozwalać`

Permission sense 1 has alternative schemas `(KOMU) + na CO`, `(KOMU) + żeby ZDANIE`, and standalone `BEZOKOLICZNIK`. It does not give one combined `(KOMU) + BEZOKOLICZNIK` schema. Inanimate enabling sense 3 separately has `na CO`, `BEZOKOLICZNIK`, and `żeby ZDANIE` without Dative. A recipient cannot be inferred from real-world permission semantics or transferred between variants. This material structure correction requires `KEEP WITH NARROWING`.

### `unikać`

The single exact schema gives direct Genitive only. Action expressions such as Genitive gerunds are nouns in the selected case, not evidence for an infinitive slot. Strong direct Genitive is retained despite weak repository evidence, while the unsupported infinitive is removed. This requires `KEEP WITH NARROWING`.

### `wymagać`

Person-requirement sense 2 and situation-requirement sense 3 each directly support Genitive, optional `od + Genitive`, and a `żeby` clause. Their subject semantics and `od` restrictions differ. `od` is directly selected, not inferred from agency, and the two senses must remain visible.

### `należeć`

Ownership sense 1 and organization-membership sense 2 independently give exact `do + Genitive`. The shared surface construction must not flatten their meanings or import category, relationship, obligation, or `należeć się` senses. Directness and practical non-motion Genitive coverage outweigh sparse repository occurrence.

### `kłócić się`

The lexical-reflexive identity directly gives optional `z + Instrumental` and optional `o + Accusative` in the singular-subject schema. A plural reciprocal subject omits the separate `z` participant. Interrogative and `że` topic clauses are alternatives. Non-reflexive `kłócić` supplies no evidence, and `się` must remain part of the lemma.

### `pokazywać`

The exact imperfective independently supports optional Dative recipient plus Accusative thing/content, exact `na + Accusative`, and an interrogative dependent clause. Optional `GDZIE` is not converted into a concrete pattern. Nothing is borrowed from `pokazać`, and incidental display locations are not treated as selected concrete government.

### `radzić sobie`

WSJP treats `radzić sobie` as the lexical entry and directly gives optional `z + Instrumental`. `sobie` is part of the identity rather than a Dative complement. Situational `w` phrases occur only in connections and receive no frame credit. The coping meaning and construction independently justify the slot alongside advice-sense `radzić`.

### `kupować`

The exact imperfective gives three alternative schemas: goods with optional seller/price, goods with optional Dative recipient, and goods with optional `dla + Genitive` beneficiary. They must not become one maximal frame. Process, habitual, and repeated buying give a `moderate` independent-slot signal, but the final two-slot opportunity-cost conclusion remains open until order 31 receives authorized Batch 4 review.
