## Fixed and multiword construction risk

`brać udział` and `wziąć udział` cannot be safely represented as ordinary instances of a generic Accusative object frame. WSJP provides fixed lexical entries whose meaning depends on required `udział` and whose participation target is expressed by `w + Locative`.

The safe later-authoring treatment is:

- keep `udział` as required lexical material;
- create a separate participation meaning/pattern for each verified lemma;
- represent governed `w + Locative` with the existing `preposition-case` type;
- keep literal taking patterns separate;
- do not introduce a fixed-expression architecture type.

Stripping `udział`, transferring participation syntax from the literal sense, or treating event collocations as valency would all corrupt the evidence.

## Meaning and boundary risks

### Purchase and transfer

The three purchase schemas are alternatives; they must not become one frame requiring seller, price, Dative beneficiary, and `dla` beneficiary together. `dawać/dać` gift occasions and purpose expressions remain adjunct-like context, not new complement slots.

### Reading

Reading place, medium, language, speed, and frequency generally remain adjuncts. Optional schema-level `GDZIE` is a selected generalized role but supplies no concrete product frame. Content-object, topic, clause, and direct-speech alternatives must remain distinguishable.

### Writing

Text creation and written correspondence are separate senses. `do + Genitive` is an addressee construction in the correspondence sense only. Creative-sense connections such as writing for a newspaper do not independently establish that addressee schema. `napisać` has schema-specific Dative optionality, including a required Dative in its dedicated clause variant; later authoring must not flatten every variant into one optional maximal frame.

### `spotykać się`

Lexical `się` must remain in the identity. `z + Instrumental` is exact with a singular personal subject, while reciprocal plural personal subjects can occur without a separate partner phrase. Frequency, time, place, and accompanying activity remain outside the complement pattern.
