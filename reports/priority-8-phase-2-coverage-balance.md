## Coverage conclusions

- The selection usefully broadens infinitive, clause, preposition + Instrumental, and non-motion preposition + Genitive research while retaining everyday object verbs.
- Direct Dative is already a relative strength, but recipient/experiencer and multi-complement teaching remains high-value rather than redundant.
- Direct Instrumental is not a quota. Treating transport vehicles as governed slots would create false balance.
- The apparent large preposition + Genitive gain is the least trustworthy raw expansion number because many provisional motion expressions may be adjuncts. Non-motion candidates must carry the reliable part of that research goal.
- `pracować jako + Nominative`, the core predicate of `czuć się`, and the ordinary price/amount construction of `kosztować` receive no coverage credit.
- No aspect partner inherits a pattern, so pair selection does not double any coverage count automatically.

## Balance lock for replacement

A replacement must first pass Phase 3 evidence and model fit. It should then repair the departing lemma's most important learner/domain contribution without worsening motion concentration, duplicating a current/selected lemma, or collapsing thin non-Accusative, clause, infinitive, lexical-`się`, or meaning-sensitive capacity. Raw matrix parity is never a selection goal.
