# Priority 8 Phase 2 — deterministic replacement policy

## Failure triggers

A selected lemma becomes replacement-eligible when Phase 3 records that the claimed useful frame is unsupported; the useful construction lies outside the locked model; the supposed complement is primarily a free adjunct; teaching value is materially weaker than expected; redundancy is substantial; the CEFR/coverage assumption materially fails; or an aspect partner offers substantially better independent value.

An uncertain single frame does not automatically remove a lemma if another independently useful, supported, architecture-compatible frame still justifies the slot.

## Ordered replacement procedure

1. Record the failed lemma, exact evidence disposition, and trigger. Do not delete it silently.
2. Describe the departing slot profile: practical domain, CEFR hypothesis, motion/reflexive/aspect-family status, learner need, and only those coverage contributions actually supported by Phase 3.
3. Filter to the 24 real Phase 0 `reserve` rows. Exclude any reserve that is unsupported, outside the locked model, above B1, duplicative of a released/remaining lemma, or dependent on inherited aspect patterns.
4. Apply hard balance constraints: keep exactly 70; keep all identities unique and non-current; do not exceed 12 motion lemmas without a human decision; normally keep each CEFR band within ±2 of 22/43/5; and do not double-slot an aspect family unless both partners independently pass.
5. Prefer candidates that repair the lost learner/domain value and protect thin non-motion Genitive, Locative, Instrumental, infinitive, clause, lexical-`się`, or multi-complement capacity. Do not preserve a coverage cell based on an adjunct.
6. Rank remaining candidates lexicographically by: (a) supported architecture-compatible governed construction, (b) everyday usefulness, (c) fit to the departing slot profile, (d) repository relevance/reuse, (e) non-redundancy and diversity, (f) lower editorial complexity. Use Phase 0 total score only as the next tie-breaker, then Phase 0 rank.
7. Document the winning replacement and rejected alternatives. Phase 3 recommends; the human owner decides in Phase 3B.

This is deterministic because the filter, constraints, ordered criteria, and final tie-breaks are explicit. It is not “take the next raw score.”

## Reserve structure

| Tier | Reserves | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A — diversity candidates | `decydować`, `wybaczać`, `rezygnować`, `marzyć`, `uczestniczyć`, `przygotowywać się`, `radzić sobie`, `odbierać`, `zostawiać`, `wybierać` | Consider after Phase 3 support when a non-motion, case/preposition, lexical-`się`, or domain gap must be repaired. |
| B — family/swap candidates | `zapytać`, `zamówić`, `zarezerwować`, `otwierać`, `otworzyć`, `zamykać`, `zamknąć`, `przychodzić`, `przyjeżdżać`, `wychodzić`, `wyjeżdżać` | Prefer for one-for-one aspect/family replacement; they do not authorize a second slot or motion increase. |
| C — architecture holds | `czuć się`, `wyglądać`, `kosztować` | Do not promote until Phase 3 supplies an independently useful representation-compatible construction and adequate evidence. Never invent a case to make them fit. |

All 24 reserves remain provisional and unverified. The seven Phase 0 `defer` rows are not replacement candidates unless a later human-authorized discovery reopening changes their status.

## Common replacement profiles

- Failed motion slot: prefer a verified non-motion Tier A candidate; a Tier B motion swap is acceptable only for materially better independent evidence and does not raise the count.
- Failed double-slotted aspect member: first test collapsing to one family slot, then fill the freed slot with a verified diversity candidate.
- Failed model-fit candidate: only an already supported locked-model reserve is eligible.
- Failed B1 candidate: prefer a verified B1 reserve when it preserves learner value, but do not sacrifice evidence quality to maintain an exact count.
- Failed simple transitive: replace with high everyday utility first, while using diversity as the tie-break rather than forcing a case quota.
