### `chodzić`

- Meanings: 2 — `regular-attendance`, `regular-on-foot-motion`.
- Patterns: 3 — `na-accusative-activity`, `do-genitive-destination`, `infinitive-purpose`; respectively activity `na + Accusative`, selected-destination `do + Genitive`, and infinitive; all A1 / active-production / core.

- Meanings: 1 — `transport-endpoint-arrival`.

- Meanings: 1 — `return-to-earlier-place`.

### `przyjść`

- Meanings: 2 — `literal-place-arrival`, `one-off-activity-arrival`.
- Patterns: 3 — `do-genitive-place-destination`, `na-accusative-event-destination`, `infinitive-purpose`; literal-place `do + Genitive`, one-off event/activity `na + Accusative`, and one-off purpose infinitive; all A2/A2 / active-production / core.

- Handling: literal place and one-off event/activity remain separate meanings. The place record excludes a person-specific target; time of arrival is absent. Destination wording does not claim exclusive `do` or `na` government.
