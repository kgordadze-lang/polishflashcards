## Locked generalized-role policy

The approved Batch 1 rule remains unchanged and governs Batch 2:

- an exact concrete `Składnia` label such as `na CO`, `o KIM/CZYM`, `KOGO/CO`, or `BEZOKOLICZNIK` directly verifies the corresponding locked-model construction;
- `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, `GDZIE`, and `KTÓRĘDY` establish selected/connoted semantic positions but not one exclusive concrete preposition;
- a documented concrete form can represent such a role only as a narrowed realization;
- the underlying generalized role must independently appear in authoritative syntax evidence; collocation alone is insufficient;
- a narrowed realization is epistemically weaker than an exact concrete frame and must be labelled accordingly;
- generalized roles do not become new architecture types.

This applies beyond motion. In `zamawiać`, optional `GDZIE` makes the provider/location position selected under WSJP methodology, while the documented `u + Genitive` provider form remains only one realization. Later batches must apply the same rule to generalized non-motion positions.
