## Finding

Adopt the corrected **21/43/6** mix. Removing A1 `nazywać się` and adding B1 `radzić sobie` changes two bands by one, within the approved ±2 guardrail. Exact preservation of 22/43/5 would require a weaker or architecture-incompatible choice. A2 remains the practical center; A1 still supplies core modals, everyday objects, residence, and foundational motion; B1 gains a practical coping/Instrumental research hypothesis.

B1 is not mechanically optimized. The released corpus already contains 11 B1 recognition patterns out of 45, and later pattern-level CEFR may classify a construction under an A2 lemma as B1 or recognition-only. Lemma and pattern distributions must not be added as though they were the same unit.

The CEFR shift follows the representation gate rather than a desire for more B1. Phase 3 should test all six B1 hypotheses early, especially low-direct-evidence `pozwalać` and `unikać` and the independent identity/value of `radzić sobie` alongside `radzić`.

## Replacement guardrail

Keep all candidates within A1–B1. Use 21/43/6 as the current provisional baseline while retaining ±2 from the original 22/43/5 target unless the human owner explicitly chooses another balance. Coverage, architecture fit, and everyday value outrank mechanical CEFR parity. These labels remain planning hypotheses until the later evidence/editorial workflow confirms them.
