## Finding

Retain the corrected 22/43/5 mix. It is intentionally not equal: A2 is the practical center for case/preposition contrasts, aspect choices, recipient frames, and everyday lexical-`się` verbs. The A1 share supplies core modals, everyday object verbs, residence, and foundational motion. The small B1 lemma share still targets high-value Dative + infinitive/clause, Genitive government, and conflict constructions.

B1 is not shown to be underrepresented merely because only five lemma labels are B1. The released corpus already contains 11 B1 recognition patterns out of 45, and later pattern-level CEFR may classify a construction under an A2 lemma as B1 or recognition-only. Lemma and pattern distributions must not be added as though they were the same unit.

No exact replacement improves the mix on Phase 2 evidence alone. Promoting a B1 reserve would trade away a current practical candidate before either side has contemporary verification. Phase 3 should test the five B1 hypotheses early, especially the low-direct-evidence `pozwalać` and `unikać`; Phase 3B can use verified B1 reserves such as `wybaczać`, `rezygnować`, `uczestniczyć`, or `radzić sobie` if needed.

## Replacement guardrail

Keep all candidates within A1–B1. Preserve the 22/43/5 target where a like-for-like verified reserve exists; otherwise remain within ±2 lemmas in each band unless the human owner explicitly chooses a different product balance. Coverage and everyday value outrank mechanical CEFR parity. These labels remain planning hypotheses until the later evidence/editorial workflow confirms them.
