# Priority 8 Phase 2 — risk review

| Risk | Current exposure | Control / Phase 3 response |
|---|---|---|
| Model incompatibility | `pracować` has an important `jako + Nominative` use outside the model; `nazywać się` has a name-predicate representation question. Architecture holds remain for `czuć się`, `kosztować`, and `wyglądać`. | Verify an independently useful compatible frame; otherwise replace. Never invent a case. |
| Complement/adjunct confusion | All 12 motion lemmas; beneficiary, location, vehicle, destination, source, route, time, and place expressions elsewhere. | Give no planning credit until exact sense-specific contemporary evidence supports selection. |
| Aspect duplication | Ten double-slotted pairs; starts/finishes have especially weak direct evidence. | Verify each partner independently and compare opportunity cost; collapse weak pairs. |
| Weak direct repository evidence | Zero exact hits for `przynosić`, `odpowiadać`, `zamawiać`, `zaczynać`, `zacząć`, `kończyć`, `pamiętać`, `zapominać`, `próbować`, `pozwalać`, `unikać`, `wymagać`, `należeć`, and `pokazywać`. | Research early. Repository context may justify a queue position but cannot establish the frame. |
| Evidence-form mismatch | `przynosić` and `pokazywać` are less directly represented than related perfective forms. | Verify the selected imperfective itself; no borrowing from a non-pool partner. |
| Meaning/clause conflation | `robić`, `brać/wziąć`, `powiedzieć`, `umówić się`, `pamiętać`, `pasować`, emotion/agreement verbs, and abstract motion senses. | Split senses and clause kinds before any frame advances. |
| Redundancy with current 30 | `martwić się` may overlap released `bać się`; non-reflexive `uczyć` is close in form to released `uczyć się`; reserve `zapytać` overlaps `pytać`. | Require distinct identity, meaning, and teaching value; inherit nothing. |
| Motion concentration | 12/70 may consume non-motion diversity even if no motion valency survives. | Hold at 12, verify early, and compare failed slots with Tier A reserves. |
| CEFR planning error | Five B1 labels rely on useful but sometimes low-evidence constructions; broad A2 lemmas may own harder patterns later. | Verify register/difficulty; keep lemma and pattern CEFR units separate. |
| Score misuse | Higher scores can still be incompatible or duplicative. | Treat score as traceability/tie-break only after gates and ordered balance criteria. |
| Reserve weakness | Reserves are discovery candidates, not pre-approved substitutes. | Apply the same contemporary evidence and model-fit gate before replacement. |
| Premature downstream work | A provisional list could be mistaken for learner-content authorization. | No stable IDs, editorial records, examples, audio, runtime edits, or final freeze before Phases 3/3B/4. |
| Report drift | Markdown/CSV/counts can diverge. | Deterministically validate set equality, counts, queue coverage, current exclusion, and path scope before commit. |

## Highest-risk Phase 3 items

1. `pracować` and `nazywać się` — architecture/model-fit boundaries.
2. The 12 motion lemmas — governed construction versus free adjunct, with `dojść` abstract versus literal senses separated.
3. `zaczynać/zacząć` and `kończyć/skończyć` — double-slot opportunity cost and weak direct evidence.
4. `przynosić` and `pokazywać` — selected forms are less directly represented than related perfectives.
5. `odpowiadać`, `zapominać`, `pozwalać`, `unikać`, and `należeć` — inclusion depends on useful constructions despite low direct repository evidence.
6. `umówić się`, `powiedzieć`, `pamiętać`, `pasować`, `martwić się`, `zgadzać się`, and `kłócić się` — meaning-sensitive or multi-complement boundaries.

These are verification priorities, not Phase 2 linguistic conclusions.
