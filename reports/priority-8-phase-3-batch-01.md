# Priority 8 Phase 3 — Batch 01 linguistic verification

## Scope and method

This report covers exactly `verification_order` 1–10 from the locked Phase 2 queue, in that order. It does not verify or recommend the syntax of any reserve or any queue row 11+.

The primary authority is WSJP PAN. Its 2026 methodology is important to the boundary analysis: the `Składnia` field records sense-specific obligatory and facultative *connoted* dependents; a parenthesized position can be omitted but follows from the lexical semantics. `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, and similar labels are generalized adverbial realizations for which one specific preposition is not required. A concrete label such as `nad CZYM`, `na CO`, or `CZYM` does identify a specific preposition/case or case form. Accordingly:

- an entry's `DOKĄD` or `SKĄD` position can establish a selected goal/source participant;
- collocations can document `do + Genitive`, `na + Accusative`, or `z + Genitive` as common realizations of that participant;
- this does **not** justify claiming that the verb exclusively governs that one preposition;
- exact concrete-frame evidence in `Składnia` and a concrete realization of generalized role evidence are not epistemically identical: the former verifies the exact construction directly, while the latter remains a narrowed realization;
- a collocation can support a narrowed realization only when `Składnia` independently establishes the underlying generalized role; collocation alone is insufficient;
- route, time, frequency, and manner material absent from `Składnia` receives no valency credit;
- the locked architecture can store concrete case or preposition-case realizations, but cannot store generalized `DOKĄD/SKĄD` as a new complement type.

The Walenty valency interface was consulted as a possible independent check but was not inspectable at lemma level through the available public-web path. No Walenty frame is claimed. Evidence levels therefore remain `A`, not `B`.

## Results

### 1. `pracować`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Is there an independently useful governed `nad + Instrumental` or other architecture-compatible frame once `jako + Nominative` and ordinary work-location adjuncts are excluded?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 5, work directed toward creating or producing something. This is distinct from employment location, occupational predicate, beneficiary, collaboration, and work-with-person senses.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `pracować`, sense 5 “nad scenariuszem”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/19959/pracowac/5068996/nad-scenariuszem), accessed 2026-08-20, gives the production/creation definition, product-oriented connections, and the exact schema `pracować + nad CZYM`.

**Supported construction.** `nad + Instrumental` in the creation/production sense maps directly to `preposition-case`.

**Rejected or unverified.** `jako + Nominative` remains outside the locked model. `w + Locative` workplace and ordinary `GDZIE` expressions receive no Verb Pattern credit. They were not used to preserve the candidate.

**Governed versus adjunct.** The `nad` object is in the exact sense's `Składnia`, so it is connoted. Workplace location is excluded as ordinary location.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** No in-pool partner is implicated. The verified frame expresses common goal-directed work and is independently useful.

**Disposition.** **KEEP WITH NARROWING** — compatible after removing the location hypothesis and the incompatible occupational predicate.

**Uncertainty.** No independently inspectable valency source corroborated the explicit WSJP schema.

### 2. `iść`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Which proposed expressions, if any, are governed for the selected meaning rather than free destination, route, time, or purpose adjuncts?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, directed motion on foot, including movement in order to perform an action.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `iść`, sense 1 “do domu”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/19012/isc/5066139/do-domu), accessed 2026-08-20, places both `DOKĄD` and `BEZOKOLICZNIK` in `Składnia`. Its connections separately show routes and destination forms.

**Supported constructions.** A selected destination participant may be concretely represented by attested `do + Genitive` or `na + Accusative` realizations, with narrowing. The explicitly selected infinitive maps directly to `infinitive`.

**Rejected or unverified.** The evidence does not establish exclusive lexical government by either `do` or `na`. Route, time, and ordinary manner remain adjuncts.

**Governed versus adjunct.** Destination and infinitive receive credit because WSJP puts them in the sense-specific schema, not because they merely occur. The generalized `DOKĄD` position is broader than either proposed preposition-case realization.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** WSJP independently describes `iść`; no syntax is borrowed from `pójść`. The directed-motion contrast and infinitival purpose construction are both practical at the hypothesized level.

**Disposition.** **KEEP VERIFIED** — at least one exact architecture-compatible construction (`infinitive`) and a selected destination position survive.

**Uncertainty.** Later content must not phrase `do + Genitive` as the only governed destination form.

### 3. `chodzić`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Does an architecture-compatible governed construction independently justify the slot after ordinary `do/na` destination and frequency adjuncts are removed?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, regular/habitual attendance or motion to an activity/place, including habitual going to perform an action.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `chodzić`, sense 1 “do szkoły”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/10552/chodzic/5123931/do-szkoly), accessed 2026-08-20, separately records optional `DOKĄD`, optional `na CO`, and optional `BEZOKOLICZNIK` in `Składnia`.

**Supported constructions.** The attendance/activity `na + Accusative` position is an exact `preposition-case` fit. The infinitive is an `infinitive` fit. A concrete `do + Genitive` record can only be a narrowed realization of generalized `DOKĄD`.

**Rejected or unverified.** Frequency receives no valency credit. Route expressions remain adjuncts. No exclusive `do` government is claimed.

**Governed versus adjunct.** The separately listed `na CO` position is stronger than an arbitrary destination co-occurrence and supplies the requested independently useful compatible construction.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** `chodzić` is independently described. The `iść/chodzić` contrast is not treated as ordinary aspect inheritance; habitual attendance also adds a concrete construction.

**Disposition.** **KEEP VERIFIED**.

**Uncertainty.** Later sense selection must distinguish attendance/activity `na CO` from purely spatial `na` destinations.

### 4. `jechać`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Is any proposed slot governed, especially the Instrumental vehicle phrase, or is the value primarily lexical motion teaching?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1.a, directed travel using a vehicle, animal, or sport equipment.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `jechać`, sense 1.a “samochodem”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/19013/jechac/5048584/samochodem), accessed 2026-08-20, defines the transport sense and gives two schemas: optional `SKĄD`, optional `DOKĄD`, and optional `CZYM`; or the same with optional `na CZYM`.

**Supported constructions.** Direct Instrumental vehicle maps to `case`. `na + Locative` equipment/vehicle maps to `preposition-case`. A `do + Genitive` destination can only be recorded as a concrete realization of generalized `DOKĄD`.

**Rejected or unverified.** Route, time, and manner outside the listed transport positions are adjuncts. No exclusive `do` government is established.

**Governed versus adjunct.** The Phase 2 suspicion that vehicle Instrumental may be merely free is not borne out by WSJP: `CZYM` is in `Składnia`. Under WSJP's stated methodology this is a facultative connoted participant, not a collocation-only inference.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** No partner evidence is borrowed. The exact transport means construction gives the slot structural value beyond the lexical determinate-motion contrast.

**Disposition.** **KEEP VERIFIED**.

**Uncertainty.** Because the selected-vehicle result reverses the Phase 2 working suspicion and Walenty could not be checked, it merits independent reviewer attention.

### 5. `jeździć`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Is any proposed case/preposition expression selected rather than a destination, route, frequency, or vehicle adjunct?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, repeated or habitual overland travel using transport.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `jeździć`, sense 1 “codziennie”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/32696/jezdzic/4647891/codziennie), accessed 2026-08-20, gives schemas with optional `SKĄD`, required `DOKĄD`, and optional `CZYM` or `na CZYM`. Frequency appears in connections, not in `Składnia`.

**Supported constructions.** Direct Instrumental vehicle maps to `case`; `na + Locative` maps to `preposition-case`; `do + Genitive` is a narrowed realization of the selected goal.

**Rejected or unverified.** Frequency and ordinary route receive no pattern credit. No exclusive `do` government is claimed.

**Governed versus adjunct.** The vehicle positions are connoted under the WSJP schema. Frequency is an adjunct despite being prominent in the sense label.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** Evidence is exact to `jeździć` and not inherited from `jechać`. The habitual/indeterminate distinction is lexical rather than an aspect pair, while the vehicle construction independently fits the product.

**Disposition.** **KEEP VERIFIED**.

**Uncertainty.** Independent review should confirm the pedagogical delimitation of selected vehicle participant versus broader manner.

### 6. `dojść`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Does contemporary evidence support the abstract result/conclusion `do + Genitive` frame, separately from literal destination motion?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 14, formulating a thought, truth, conclusion, or conviction on some basis.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `dojść`, sense 14 “do prawdy”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/11867/dojsc/5100578/do-prawdy), accessed 2026-08-20, isolates the intellectual sense and explicitly gives `dojść + do CZEGO`.

**Supported construction.** Abstract-result `do + Genitive` maps exactly to `preposition-case`.

**Rejected or unverified.** Literal walking destinations are not used to license this abstract frame, and the frame is not generalized across other `dojść` senses.

**Governed versus adjunct.** This is an exact concrete preposition/case in the sense-specific schema, not a free motion destination.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** The source independently describes perfective `dojść`; no schema is copied from `dochodzić`. The conclusion/result construction is common, meaning-specific, and highly teachable.

**Disposition.** **KEEP VERIFIED**.

**Uncertainty.** The lexical noun range should remain within attested thought/result classes.

### 7. `dojechać`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Is the endpoint expression governed/selective, and is Instrumental vehicle only a free adjunct?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, reaching an endpoint by travelling in or on transport.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `dojechać`, sense 1 “na miejsce”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/32149/dojechac/5020238/na-miejsce), accessed 2026-08-20, gives optional `DOKĄD` plus optional `CZYM` or `na CZYM` for human subjects.

**Supported constructions.** A concrete endpoint may be represented as an attested `do + Genitive` or `na + Accusative` realization of `DOKĄD`. Direct Instrumental vehicle maps to `case`; `na + Locative` maps to `preposition-case`.

**Rejected or unverified.** Route and time remain adjuncts. There is no evidence that `do` is the exclusive endpoint preposition.

**Governed versus adjunct.** Endpoint is a facultative connoted position. Contrary to the Phase 2 suspicion, WSJP also analyses vehicle as facultatively connoted in this exact sense.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** The perfective endpoint meaning has exact-lemma evidence. Its case-compatible transport construction and bounded endpoint are practical.

**Disposition.** **KEEP VERIFIED**.

**Uncertainty.** The unexpected vehicle finding needs independent review because no second lemma-level valency source was accessible.

### 8. `wracać`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Does either proposed `do/z + Genitive` expression qualify as governed for the scoped return meaning rather than a goal/source adjunct?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, returning again to a place previously occupied.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `wracać`, sense 1 “do kraju”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/29026/wracac/4998113/do-kraju), accessed 2026-08-20, gives required `DOKĄD` plus optional `SKĄD`; the connections document both `do`-goal and `z`-source forms.

**Supported constructions.** `do + Genitive` and `z + Genitive` are architecture-compatible realizations of selected goal/source positions.

**Rejected or unverified.** Neither preposition is the only possible realization. Time, manner, and vehicle expressions are not in this schema and receive no credit.

**Governed versus adjunct.** The goal and source are connoted semantic participants under WSJP, not arbitrary co-occurrences. The report deliberately calls the specific forms *realizations*, not exclusive lexical government.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** The imperfective entry is independently evidenced. Ongoing/repeated return is practical, but constructionally duplicates the perfective partner.

**Disposition.** **KEEP WITH NARROWING** — the lemma remains viable through concrete `do/z + Genitive` realizations of selected `DOKĄD/SKĄD`, without treating those realizations as exact exclusive government.

**Uncertainty.** Later authoring must not collapse generalized goal/source selection into a claim that the verb uniquely governs `do` and `z`.

### 9. `wrócić`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Does this perfective independently justify a slot and support any governed complement not inherited from `wracać`?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, bounded return to a place previously occupied.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `wrócić`, sense 1 “do kraju”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/37967/wrocic/5124719/do-kraju), accessed 2026-08-20, independently records required `DOKĄD` and optional `SKĄD`, with `do`-goal and `z`-source connections.

**Supported constructions.** `do + Genitive` and `z + Genitive` are concrete `preposition-case` realizations of the selected positions.

**Rejected or unverified.** No complement unique to the perfective was found. Time and manner remain adjuncts; no exclusive preposition government is claimed.

**Governed versus adjunct.** Exact-lemma evidence supports selected goal/source positions. Nothing is inherited from the imperfective entry.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** The bounded perfective meaning is independently common and teachable, but the separate-slot signal is only **moderate** because it adds aspect value without adding construction coverage.

**Disposition.** **KEEP WITH NARROWING** provisionally — concrete `do/z + Genitive` forms remain viable narrowed realizations of selected `DOKĄD/SKĄD`, not exact concrete requirements in `Składnia`. This does not settle the later pair-level opportunity-cost decision.

**Uncertainty.** Phase 3B should compare the cost of double-slotting this pair only after reserve candidates themselves are verified.

### 10. `przyjść`

**Phase 2 hypothesis/question.** Which proposed event/person expressions are governed rather than ordinary arrival destination/time adjuncts?

**Senses researched.** WSJP sense 1, literal arrival at a place, and sense 2, arrival to perform or undergo a one-off activity. The latter is distinct from time of arrival and from non-motion senses.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `przyjść`, sense 1 “do domu”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/18526/przyjsc/4972241/do-domu), accessed 2026-08-20, gives `DOKĄD`. [WSJP PAN, `przyjść`, sense 2 “na kolację”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/18526/przyjsc/4972242/na-kolacje), accessed 2026-08-20, documents event/activity `na` forms and gives `DOKĄD` plus `BEZOKOLICZNIK`.

**Supported constructions.** Place `do + Genitive` and event/activity `na + Accusative` are narrowed realizations of selected `DOKĄD`; the purpose infinitive maps exactly to `infinitive`.

**Rejected or unverified.** The exact entries inspected do not establish a distinct person-specific `do + Genitive` complement. Time remains an adjunct, and no exclusive `do/na` government is claimed.

**Governed versus adjunct.** Event/activity destination belongs to a separately defined purpose sense and is represented in its connections and schema. The infinitive is explicitly selected. Person destination lacks equally exact support in the inspected entry.

**Aspect independence and learner value.** Evidence is exact to perfective `przyjść`; none is borrowed from reserve `przychodzić`. The one-off purpose/activity construction has strong practical value.

**Disposition.** **KEEP WITH NARROWING** — remove the person-specific hypothesis; retain event/activity and infinitive constructions.

**Uncertainty.** Later authoring must preserve the event/location boundary and cannot restore the person frame without new evidence.
