## Method

The synthesis keeps two axes separate:

1. **Linguistic disposition** asks whether the exact lemma and scoped meaning have authoritative contemporary evidence compatible with the locked `case`, `preposition-case`, `infinitive`, and `clause` architecture. The seven approved Batch CSVs remain authoritative.
2. **Product allocation** asks whether a linguistically valid lemma merits one of approximately 70 added slots after aspect overlap, motion concentration, surviving value after narrowing, released-lemma overlap, and diversity opportunity cost are considered.

Generalized `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, and `GDZIE` positions are selected semantic roles, not new architecture types and not proof of one exclusive preposition. A documented concrete `do`, `na`, `z`, or `w` form may be carried forward only as an explicitly labelled realization. Exact concrete `Składnia` labels and generalized-role evidence remain epistemically distinct.

The mechanical base is: 7 Batch CSVs × 10 rows; orders 1–70 exactly; 70 unique lemmas; width 35 for every header and data row; 49 `compatible`, 21 `compatible with narrowing`; 57 strong and 13 moderate independent-slot signals; evidence `A` for all 70.

## Motion-verb balance

**Motion balance: `BORDERLINE`.** The verified set contains **12/70 motion lemmas (17.1%)**, matching the Phase 2 ceiling. All 12 have independent grade-A evidence. Six are `KEEP VERIFIED` (`iść`, `chodzić`, `jechać`, `jeździć`, `dojść`, `dojechać`) and six are `KEEP WITH NARROWING` (`wracać`, `wrócić`, `przyjść`, `przyjechać`, `wyjść`, `wyjechać`).

The block is pedagogically defensible: it covers determinate/indeterminate on-foot and vehicle motion, exact attendance and infinitive uses, selected vehicle positions, abstract `dojść do + Genitive`, bounded endpoint, return, arrival, exit, and departure. These are useful central Polish distinctions, not merely twelve copies of “go.”

The balance is not `GOOD` because generalized goal/source realizations recur, the `wracać / wrócić` pair consumes two slots without unique construction coverage, and `przyjechać` / `wyjechać` survive more narrowly with moderate signals. It is not `OVERCONCENTRATED` because every lemma passed the exact-lemma evidence gate and several add distinct constructions or lexical motion contrasts. Phase 3B should approve or reduce the ceiling deliberately; no motion lemma is removed here.

## Phase 4 authoring constraints carried forward

- Treat generalized `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, and `GDZIE` as selected roles, not new product types or proof of exclusive `do/na/z/w` government; label concrete forms as realizations.
- Author sense-specific schemas. Do not merge polysemy because surface morphology matches.
- Keep alternative schemas alternative; never create an unsupported maximal frame by combining every optional participant and complement.
- Preserve lexical `się` and fixed lexical identity, including `radzić sobie`.
- Preserve required lexical material such as `udział` in `brać/wziąć udział w + Locative`; do not reduce the fixed construction to generic object syntax.
- Keep aspect-partner evidence independent. Partner metadata may teach a relationship but does not license inherited syntax.
- Treat ordinary Genitive under negation as grammar-owned, not a lemma-specific pattern such as `jeść + Genitive`.
- Preserve exact clause distinctions: `że`, `żeby`, interrogative-dependent, and direct speech are not freely interchangeable.
- Keep related lexical identities separate: `radzić` / `radzić sobie`, `uczyć` / `uczyć się`, reflexive/non-reflexive entries, and inspected/non-inspected aspect partners.
- Keep exact concrete frames epistemically distinct from generalized-role realizations, collocations, adjuncts, and unsupported participant inferences.
