# Priority 8 Phase 2 — selection policy

## Decision and scope

Phase 2 retains the corrected Phase 0 `include` set as an exact **70-lemma provisional research queue**. It does not approve a lemma linguistically, approve any proposed frame, create learner wording, or freeze final identities. Phase 3 must verify every row; Phase 3B remains the human lemma freeze.

The only eligible universe is the 101 non-current lemmas in `priority-8-candidate-verbs.csv`. The 30 released lemmas are a hard exclusion. The locked complement vocabulary is `case`, `preposition-case`, `infinitive`, and `clause`; no candidate receives an invented case merely to pass that gate.

## Reproducible decision sequence

Apply these steps in order. Later steps cannot waive an earlier gate.

1. **Identity gate:** require a unique lemma in the Phase 0 pool and exclude every released lemma.
2. **Scope gate:** require plausible A1–B1 practical use and a researchable learner need. A CEFR label is a planning hypothesis, not proof.
3. **Representation-fit gate:** classify the most useful proposed construction as `compatible`, `probably compatible - verify`, `model-fit concern`, or, for reserves, `reserve pending architecture evidence`. A model-fit concern may remain provisional only when another useful compatible hypothesis could independently justify research.
4. **Evidence gate:** record repository occurrence/reuse as Po polsku relevance only. It cannot establish valency. Absence of exact infinitive hits raises Phase 3 priority but is not an automatic rejection when a documented repository context and a strong learner gap exist.
5. **Independent-value gate:** assess everyday usefulness, construction-learning need, and non-duplicative teaching value. For aspect partners, apply this gate to each lemma separately.
6. **Balance pass:** compare CEFR, construction diversity, lexical `się`, meaning sensitivity, aspect duplication, motion concentration, redundancy with the current 30, redundancy within the 70, editorial complexity, and opportunity cost.
7. **Size lock:** choose exactly 70 and keep real reserves. Phase 2 changes a corrected Phase 0 inclusion only if internal repository evidence contradicts its identity, evidence summary, or model-fit disposition. No such contradiction was found.
8. **Risk ordering:** queue model-fit concerns first, then motion/adjunct risks, zero-exact-hit candidates, double-slotted aspect pairs, meaning-sensitive candidates, and routine confirmations.

## Positive selection signals

- practical everyday usefulness and A1–B1 learner relevance;
- specific committed repository contexts or plausible exact reuse;
- a clear learner need for governed case, preposition, infinitive, or clause knowledge;
- likely contribution to a thin construction area without manufacturing coverage;
- meaning contrasts that are useful and teachable selectively;
- independent aspect or lexical-`się` value;
- reasonable deterministic exercise and editorial scope.

These signals justify research priority only. They do not prove a Polish construction.

## Balancing constraints

- exactly 70 new unique lemmas and no overlap with the released 30;
- retain an A1–B1 practical center rather than equal CEFR proportions;
- do not let ordinary Accusative transitives crowd out harder government;
- cap the provisional motion set at 12 unless a human explicitly accepts a different trade-off;
- double-slot an aspect pair only when both partners provisionally clear the independent-value and opportunity-cost tests;
- count only architecture-compatible coverage opportunities; destination, source, route, time, place, vehicle, and beneficiary adjuncts receive no automatic credit;
- preserve meaningful non-motion, Dative, Genitive, clause, infinitive, lexical-`się`, and meaning-sensitive research capacity.

The locked lemma-level planning target is 22 A1, 43 A2, and 5 B1. A replacement should normally keep each band within two lemmas of that target and must remain A1–B1. Pattern-level CEFR is decided later and may differ from lemma-level planning metadata.

## Exclusion and defer signals

- the useful core construction lies outside the locked model and no independently useful compatible frame is documented;
- the apparent frame is primarily a free adjunct;
- overlap with a released lemma or another selected lemma consumes a slot without independent teaching value;
- weak repository relevance plus low practical priority;
- advanced, formal, marginal, or overly polysemous scope disproportionate to A1–B1 value;
- an aspect partner relies on inheritance rather than its own evidence;
- editorial or representation complexity outweighs the candidate's marginal benefit.

`czuć się` and `kosztować` therefore remain architecture holds. Their ordinary useful constructions must not be relabelled to force compatibility.

## Phase 3 verification questions

Phase 3 must determine, with contemporary evidence, whether the scoped meaning supports the proposed governed complement; whether an expression is lexical selection or a free adjunct; whether the useful frame fits the locked model; whether meaning and clause kinds are correctly separated; whether each aspect partner independently deserves a slot; and whether the planning CEFR/register assumptions hold.

Phase 2 deliberately does not answer those questions from model intuition.

## Meaning of the Phase 0 score

The Phase 0 `total_score` is the documented sum of positive planning components minus overlap, editorial, meaning, pattern, aspect/reflexive, and audio costs. Its component scales remain those in `priority-8-100-lemma-candidate-pool.md`. Phase 2 preserves the number for traceability and does not recalculate it.

The score means **decision support inside Phase 0 discovery**. It does not mean linguistic probability, corpus frequency, valency confidence, CEFR certification, architecture approval, final rank, or product approval. It is not the replacement order. The retained corrections (`przynosić` and `kłócić się` over `czuć się` and `kosztować`) demonstrate that explicit gates and balance judgments override raw totals.
