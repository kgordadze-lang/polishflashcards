## Dictionary-schema interpretation

The current WSJP methodology resolves part of the apparent ambiguity:

- `Składnia` contains obligatory and facultative connoted dependents whose presence follows from the exact sense's semantics.
- Parentheses mean optional, not free.
- `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, `GDZIE`, and `KTÓRĘDY` are used when a position admits prepositional realizations without requiring one particular preposition.
- A concrete label such as `nad CZYM`, `na CO`, or `CZYM` supports the exact preposition/case or nominal case.

The product-model risk follows directly: a concrete `do + Genitive` card can honestly instantiate selected `DOKĄD`, but must not state or imply that `do` is the verb's sole lexically governed form. All such rows are marked `compatible with narrowing`.

This policy is cumulative for later batches:

- a concrete `Składnia` label such as `nad CZYM`, `na CO`, `CZYM`, or `do CZEGO` directly verifies that exact locked-model construction;
- generalized `DOKĄD`, `SKĄD`, `GDZIE`, or `KTÓRĘDY` verifies a selected/connoted semantic position but not one unique preposition;
- an attested concrete preposition-case form may represent that generalized role only as a narrowed realization;
- the underlying role must be independently selected in `Składnia`; a collocation alone is insufficient;
- exact concrete-frame evidence and narrowed realization evidence are not epistemically identical;
- no generalized role becomes a new architecture type, and no narrowed realization may be described as exclusive lexical government.

## Sense ambiguity

- `pracować`: production `nad + Instrumental` must remain separate from employment location, occupational `jako` predicate, work for a beneficiary, and work with people.
- `chodzić`: attendance/activity `na + Accusative` must remain separate from arbitrary spatial `na` destinations.
- `dojść`: intellectual result/conclusion sense 14 must remain separate from literal arrival and all other abstract senses.
- `przyjść`: literal arrival sense 1 and one-off purpose/activity sense 2 must remain separate; person destination was not established by the exact entries inspected.
- `wracać/wrócić`: physical return sense 1 was used. Abstract return-to-topic/activity senses were not merged into it.

## Adjunct risks

- `iść`: route, time, and ordinary manner are adjuncts. Infinitive receives credit only because it appears in `Składnia`.
- `chodzić`: frequency and route are adjuncts.
- `jechać/jeździć`: route, time, and frequency remain adjuncts. Vehicle receives credit only within the exact transport senses and only because `CZYM/na CZYM` appears in `Składnia`.
- `dojechać`: route and time are adjuncts. Endpoint and vehicle are facultative connoted positions under WSJP.
- `wracać/wrócić`: time, manner, and vehicle receive no credit in the physical-return schema. Goal/source do receive selected-participant credit, but `do/z` are merely concrete realizations.
- `przyjść`: arrival time is adjunct. The event/activity and infinitive constructions belong to the separately defined purpose sense.

No motion expression was credited solely from an occurrence or collocation.

## Vehicle-position review risk

The most review-sensitive result is that WSJP places direct Instrumental `CZYM` and/or `na CZYM` inside the syntax schemas for `jechać`, `jeździć`, and `dojechać`. Under WSJP's own methodology these are optional connoted dependents, not free adjuncts. This reverses the Phase 2 working suspicion for vehicle phrases.

Recommended independent review question: does the Po polsku Verb Patterns editorial standard accept WSJP's facultative connotation analysis as sufficient selection for a learner pattern, or does the project impose a stricter complement/adjunct cutoff for vehicle roles? Phase 3 does not change the standard; it flags the issue and preserves the source's analysis accurately.
