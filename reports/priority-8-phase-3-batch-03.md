### 21. `zapominać`

**Phase 2 question.** Does contemporary evidence support the proposed o + Locative, Genitive object, and żeby-clause scopes despite weak direct repository evidence?

**Senses researched.** WSJP sense 1, losing recall of people, things, events, or information; separately, sense 4, failing to perform an action that should have been performed.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `zapominać`, sense 1 “o kłopotach”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/16991/zapominac/3776885/o-klopotach), accessed 2026-08-20, gives `KOGO/CZEGO | KOGO/CO`, `o KIM/CZYM`, and `że ZDANIE | ZDANIE PYTAJNOZALEŻNE`. [WSJP PAN, sense 4 “podziękować”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/16991/zapominac/3776888/podziekowac), accessed 2026-08-20, separately gives `BEZOKOLICZNIK`.

**Phase 2 answer.** Partly. Exact evidence supports `o + Locative` and a direct Genitive/Accusative object in the recall sense. It also directly supports recall-sense `że` and interrogative clauses plus a separate obligation-sense infinitive. Neither inspected schema lists the proposed `żeby` clause.

**Construction, boundary, and value.** Recall-sense Genitive/Accusative maps to `case`, `o + Locative` to `preposition-case`, and both listed clause types to `clause`. The distinct failure-to-act infinitive maps to `infinitive`. Time, duration, cause, and manner are not credited, and the two sense inventories are not made interchangeable. The everyday meanings and breadth of direct evidence provide strong slot value despite the clause correction.

**Representation and independence.** `compatible with narrowing`. Evidence is exact to imperfective `zapominać`; no aspect partner or reserve supplied syntax.

**Disposition.** **KEEP WITH NARROWING**.

**Uncertainty.** Later authoring must keep recall and failure-to-act meanings separate and must not substitute a `żeby` clause for the verified infinitive.

### 23. `pozwalać`

**Phase 2 question.** Does contemporary evidence support Dative person + infinitive and/or na + Accusative in a practical B1 scope despite low direct repository evidence?

**Senses researched.** WSJP sense 1, human permission or non-prevention; separately, sense 3, an inanimate thing enabling an action or result.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `pozwalać`, sense 1 “komuś wyjść”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/23683/pozwalac/4778958/komus-wyjsc), accessed 2026-08-20, gives `(KOMU) + na CO`, `(KOMU) + żeby ZDANIE`, and a separate `BEZOKOLICZNIK` schema. [WSJP PAN, sense 3 “na dokonanie obserwacji”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/23683/pozwalac/4778959/na-dokonanie-obserwacji), accessed 2026-08-20, separately gives `na CO`, `BEZOKOLICZNIK`, and `żeby ZDANIE`, without Dative.

**Phase 2 answer.** Partly. Exact evidence supports `na + Accusative`, a separate infinitive, and useful `żeby`-clause variants. Optional Dative is explicit with the permission-sense `na` and `żeby` variants, but WSJP does not present Dative person + infinitive as one combined schema.

**Construction, boundary, and value.** Dative maps to `case`, `na + Accusative` to `preposition-case`, the bare infinitive to `infinitive`, and `żeby` to `clause`. The variants are alternatives, not a single freely combinable frame. A real-world permission recipient cannot add Dative to the infinitive schema, and no Dative transfers to the inanimate enabling sense. The permission and enabling constructions retain strong practical B1 value.

**Representation and independence.** `compatible with narrowing`. Evidence is exact to imperfective `pozwalać`; none is inherited from `pozwolić`.

**Disposition.** **KEEP WITH NARROWING**.

**Uncertainty.** Later authoring must expose the alternative schemas and their subject/participant restrictions.
