### 51. `powiedzieć`

**Phase 2 question.** Which recipient, object, że-clause, and żeby-clause combinations are governed in distinct scoped meanings rather than collapsed polysemy?

**Sense researched.** WSJP sense 1, spoken communication that conveys interpretable content.

**Authoritative evidence.** [WSJP PAN, `powiedzieć`, sense 1 “o miłości”](https://wsjp.pl/haslo/podglad/8100/powiedziec/5157825/o-milosci), accessed 2026-08-21, lists seven separate schemas: optional Dative or `do + Genitive` addressee with Accusative content; optional Dative or `do + Genitive` addressee with `o + Locative` topic; optional Dative or `do + Genitive` addressee with a `że`, `żeby`, or interrogative-dependent clause; and direct speech alone.

**Complete direct constructions.** Dative addressee and Accusative content map to `case`. `do + Genitive` addressee and `o + Locative` topic map to `preposition-case`. `że`, `żeby`, interrogative-dependent content, and direct speech map to `clause`. Directly listed `JAK` lies outside the locked four types and creates no new type.

**Phase 2 answer.** Yes. One broad spoken-communication sense directly supplies several alternative recipient/content schemas. Dative and `do` addressees are alternatives; object, topic, content clause, and direct speech are alternative content shapes. They never become one `KOMU + CO + o + że + żeby` template.

**Boundary and rejected hypotheses.** Voice, manner, channel, location, time, and communicative circumstances remain adjuncts. Direct speech is not assigned a recipient absent from its exact schema. Nonverbal and other unrelated meanings are excluded.

**Representation, identity, value, and disposition.** `compatible`; exact perfective lemma; **strong** independent-slot signal; **KEEP VERIFIED**.

**Uncertainty.** Later authoring must label the communicative relation and preserve the alternative schemas; `JAK` remains unrepresented under the locked architecture.
