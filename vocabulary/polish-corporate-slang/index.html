Polish corporate slang - office Polish decoded

Office and corporate slang for open-space life and team projects.

B1

korpo

the corporate world
A big corporation or corporate life in general. Slightly weary tone. Office slang - it would not appear in formal writing.
Pracuję w korpo od pięciu lat.
I've worked in the corporate world for five years.

fakap

a screw-up
From English 'f-up', declined like a normal Polish noun. Office slang only - and it still carries the original obscenity.
Mieliśmy wczoraj niezły fakap na produkcji.
We had a pretty big screw-up in production yesterday.

dedlajn

deadline
The English word, respelled Polish-style. Office jargon; in neutral Polish say 'termin'.
Dedlajn jest jutro o dwunastej.
The deadline is tomorrow at noon.

asapem

ASAP
English acronym bent into a Polish adverb (instrumental case). Office jargon; neutral Polish would be 'jak najszybciej'.
Wyślij mi ten plik asapem.
Send me that file ASAP.

mityng

a meeting
Respelled 'meeting'. 'Wskoczyć na mityng' = jump on a call. Office jargon; the ordinary word is 'spotkanie'.
Mam mityng o dziesiątej.
I've got a meeting at ten.

ficzer

a feature
Product/dev slang from English 'feature'. Team jargon; neutral Polish is 'funkcja'.
Wypuszczamy nowy ficzer w przyszłym tygodniu.
We're shipping a new feature next week.

harować

to slave away
Aspect pair: harować (impf only)
To work exhaustingly hard.
Haruję nad tym raportem od rana.
I've been slaving over this report since morning.

tyrać

to toil
Aspect pair: tyrać (impf only)
Near-synonym of 'harować', equally casual. A woman says: Tyrałam.
Tyrałem nad tym przez cały weekend.
I toiled over this all weekend.

ogarnąć temat

to handle it
Aspect pair: ogarniać / ogarnąć temat
To take care of a task or get on top of an issue.
Spokojnie, ogarnę ten temat do piątku.
Relax, I'll handle this by Friday.

Mordor

Mordor (Warsaw's Służewiec office district)
The office-tower zone around Domaniewska in Warsaw's Służewiec, named after Tolkien's Mordor for its traffic and crowds. It names that district - it is not a general Polish word for any business district.
Znowu straszne korki w drodze do Mordoru.
Terrible traffic on the way to Mordor again.

L4

sick leave
Pronounced 'el-cztery'. Colloquial name for a doctor's sick note.
Czuję się fatalnie, biorę L4.
I feel awful, I'm taking sick leave.

urlop na żądanie

leave on demand
A real labour-law entitlement, in slang just 'użet' (UŻ). A woman says: Zaspałam.
Zaspałem, biorę urlop na żądanie.
I overslept, I'm taking leave on demand.
